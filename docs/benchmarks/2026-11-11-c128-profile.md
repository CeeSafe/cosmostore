## Conclusions

- G30 costs nothing measurable at staging at concurrency 128.
- Zeroing and the signing key were 19% of the event loop; ADR-0035 removes most of both.
- Small reads have a tail of 20 to 30 ms against a p50 of 1 to 5 ms. It is a stall, not the requests' cost, and
  is the next thing to find.
